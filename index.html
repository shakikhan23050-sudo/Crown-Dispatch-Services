From sign-up to booked load

Five steps, in this order. We check who we are working with before we book anything.

  1. 1

    You apply

    Fill in the carrier form or message us on WhatsApp. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 2

    We check your authority

    We look up your USDOT and MC numbers in the public FMCSA records, and ask for your insurance certificate and W-9.

  3. 3

    You review the agreement

    We send a short dispatch agreement with the fee written in. Nothing starts until you sign it.

  4. 4

    We find and negotiate loads

    We search for loads that fit your truck, your lanes and your minimum rate, and negotiate with brokers. You approve each load before we book it.

  5. 5

    We follow up on the paperwork

    You get the rate confirmation to review. We follow up with the broker and keep a record of every load we book for you.

Questions carriers ask

What does a truck dispatcher do?

A dispatcher finds loads for your truck, negotiates the rate with the broker and handles the follow-up so you can focus on driving. You keep your own authority and you make the final call on every load.

How much does it cost?

We charge a percentage of each load we book for you. There are no upfront fees. The exact percentage goes in the dispatch agreement before you sign anything.

Do I need my own MC authority and insurance?

Yes. You need active USDOT and MC numbers and liability insurance on file. We check them in the public FMCSA records before we book a load for you.

Do you guarantee loads or income?

No. Freight availability and rates change every day, and we cannot promise a specific number of loads or a specific income.

Are you a freight broker?

No. We are a dispatch service that works for the carrier. We do not take possession of freight and we do not hold your money.

How long until I hear back?

We reply to every sign-up within 1 to 2 business days, by email or WhatsApp.

How do you use my information?

Only to contact you about dispatch services. Please do not send your Social Security number, bank details or card numbers through this site.
